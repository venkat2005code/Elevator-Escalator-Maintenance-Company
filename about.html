Who We Are

Precision Engineers, Trusted Partners

Founded in 2001, VertEx has grown from a five-person team to one of the region's most respected vertical transport specialists. We serve over 500 commercial buildings, hospitals, hotels, and public infrastructure projects.

Our approach is simple: combine certified engineering talent with data-driven maintenance programs to achieve uptime levels the industry thought impossible.
