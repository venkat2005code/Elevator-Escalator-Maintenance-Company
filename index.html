Escalator Maintenance Services

Complete Escalator Care — From Inspection to Overhaul

Our factory-trained technicians service all major escalator brands — Otis, KONE, Schindler, ThyssenKrupp — keeping your units safe, smooth, and code-compliant.

Routine Lubrication & Adjustment
Regular greasing of step chains, combs, and drive components to minimise wear and extend unit lifespan beyond manufacturer benchmarks.
Drive System Overhaul
Full inspection and replacement of motors, gearboxes, and variable frequency drives (VFDs) to restore peak performance and energy efficiency.
Step & Handrail Replacement
On-site replacement of cracked steps, worn treads, and deteriorated handrail belts — keeping surfaces safe and aesthetically immaculate.
Controller Modernization
Upgrade legacy relay logic to modern PLC-based controllers with remote diagnostics, fault logging, and real-time energy reporting.
Skirt Panel & Comb Repair
Replacement of worn skirt panels and broken comb teeth — critical safety components that prevent passenger entrapment incidents.
IoT Remote Monitoring
24/7 sensor-based monitoring of escalator health data, triggering automated dispatch before minor faults escalate into costly breakdowns.

Inspection & Safety Checks

Full Regulatory Compliance — Every Time

Our accredited inspection teams carry out thorough statutory checks in line with Dubai Municipality (DM), DEWA, and international EN 81 / ASME A17.1 standards.

Annual Statutory Inspection
Mandatory government-required annual inspections with official certification issued within 5 working days of a passed inspection.
Load & Safety Device Testing
Rated load testing, governor rope checks, buffer impact tests, and full safety chain verification to ASME A17.1 standards.
Fire Recall & Evacuation Mode Test
Verification of Phase I and Phase II firefighter emergency operation as mandated by local civil defence requirements.
Door Operation & Entrapment Check
Full door cycle testing including reopening force, closing speed, nudging mode, and mechanical interlock integrity assessments.
Energy Performance Audit
Power consumption benchmarking and recommendations to optimise VFD settings, reducing your building's energy costs.
Compliance Certificate Issuance
Digital compliance certificates delivered via client portal — instantly available for building audits, insurance renewals, and permit applications.
