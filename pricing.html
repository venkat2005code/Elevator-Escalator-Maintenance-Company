Common Questions

FAQ

Are contracts locking me in for years?

We offer flexible 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year contracts. Longer terms include discounted monthly rates and price-lock guarantees.

What happens if a part is discontinued?

Our extensive reverse-engineering and modernization department can retrofit modern controllers to legacy systems without requiring a full shaft tear-out.

Do you service equipment installed by other companies?

Yes. Our technicians are cross-trained on all major OEMs including Otis, Schindler, KONE, and ThyssenKrupp.
